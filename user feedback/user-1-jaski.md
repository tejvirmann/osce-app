## OSCE Structure (Their Program)

- 4 clinical skills classes total
- 1 class focused on physical exam
- Cases organized by organ system/unit

### Case Topics
1. Organ-specific problems (e.g., no periods / erectile dysfunction → Reproductive Endocrinology)
2. Intimate Partner Violence (IPV)
3. Sexual history
4. 4th case — TBD / unknown to student in advance

---

## Feature Requests

### Case Randomization
- Cases should be randomized — student doesn't know which scenario they'll get in advance
- Mirrors real OSCE conditions

### Differential Diagnosis System
- Each case should have a list of possible conditions (differentials)
- The simulator picks one and generates matching symptoms
- Student must work through the case and arrive at the differential
- Example: "patient hasn't had periods" → list of causes → simulator picks one → student figures it out

### IPV Case Difficulty Levels
- **Easy IPV:** patient openly discloses
- **Hard IPV:** patient hasn't realized they're experiencing it, or hasn't disclosed yet

### Hint System
- Provide hints during the case if the student is stuck

### Concise Mode
- Give students the option to receive concise summaries/feedback (vs. detailed)

### Clinical Skill Documents & Summaries
- Attach relevant clinical skill reference documents to each case
- Post-case summary of what was covered

### End-of-Case Grading
- Grade the student at the end
- Include professionalism score
- Timed — simulate real OSCE time pressure

---

## Key Insight
> Students are trained on 4 specific cases per unit. The simulator should be able to generate any of those cases randomly, so students can't predict what's coming — just like the real exam.
